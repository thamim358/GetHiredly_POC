Privacy Policy

🔒 100% Local Data Storage Guarantee

Your data never leaves your computer. LinkedIn AutoBot stores all information locally on your system. We do not use external databases, cloud storage, or remote servers for your personal data.

1. Local Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your computer:

  • LinkedIn credentials are stored in local configuration files
  • Gmail/email credentials remain on your system
  • Resume files are stored in local directories
  • Application history is saved to local CSV files
  • Job configurations are stored as local JSON files
  • No external databases or cloud storage are used

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you provide directly through the application interface:

  • Your name and contact information for email templates
  • LinkedIn credentials for automation (stored locally)
  • Gmail credentials for sending applications (stored locally)
  • Resume files and job preferences (stored locally)

Important: This information is processed and stored entirely on your local machine.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:

  • Automating LinkedIn job searches on your behalf
  • Extracting hiring manager emails from job posts
  • Sending personalized job applications
  • Tracking application history locally

All processing happens locally on your computer.

4. Information Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone.

  • No data is transmitted to Evolisk servers
  • No external databases store your information
  • No third-party services have access to your data
  • No analytics or tracking services collect your information

5. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your computer:

  • You have complete control over your data security
  • Data is protected by your computer's security measures
  • No external network vulnerabilities
  • You can delete all data at any time by removing the application

6. Your Rights

Since your data is stored locally, you have complete control:

  • View all stored data in local files
  • Modify or delete any information
  • Export your data (it's already on your computer)
  • No need to request data deletion from us

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@evolisk.com. However, remember that we cannot access your data since it's stored locally on your computer.
